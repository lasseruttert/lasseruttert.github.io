Lasse Ruttert

AI safety research at the University of Bonn.

I want to see AI take my job, so that I can live on a farm in the mountains with my 6 dogs and 3 cats.

Currently, I am a graduate research assistant at the University of Bonn, working on AI safety, and an M.Sc. student in Computer Science there. My interests are RL, NLP, agents and their harnesses, and the intersection of all of them.

I want to see AI make a positive impact on the world in a safe and controlled way, and I want to take part in shaping that future.

Besides my research interests, I love to go hiking, especially in the Alps, to climb up some funny, colorful rocks, and I feel good when I pass an old couple on their E-bike while I am sweating (a lot).

You will also find me sitting in a café with my friends or on basically any flea market ever.

Have fun looking around :)
